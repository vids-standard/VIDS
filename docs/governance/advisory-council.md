# Announcing the Inaugural Members of the VIDS Advisory Council

Princeton Medical Systems is pleased to announce the inaugural members of the **VIDS Advisory Council**, an independent advisory body supporting the development of the Verified Imaging Dataset Standard (VIDS).

VIDS is an open standard for structured, machine-checkable documentation of medical imaging datasets used in AI/ML development. The Advisory Council helps keep the standard grounded in clinical, research, and technical practice by bringing independent perspectives from experts who work with medical imaging data and AI.

Council members serve in their individual capacity. **The Council is advisory and does not set or approve VIDS conformance requirements.**

Vera Damerjian Pieters, PhD, is the founder of VeraDP, an R&D leadership and advisory practice specializing in medical imaging AI.
